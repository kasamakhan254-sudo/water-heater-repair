Common Water Heater Problems

Water heaters may produce insufficient hot water, make unusual noises, or show signs of leaking. The cause depends on the equipment and its condition.

If you notice a gas smell, leave the area and contact the appropriate emergency or gas service. Do not attempt dangerous repairs yourself.

Water Heater Installation

Choosing a water heater depends on household hot water needs, energy source, available space, and local building requirements. Have installation completed by a qualified professional.

Water Heater Maintenance

Regular professional inspections can help identify wear, leaks, and other issues early. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance instructions for your specific model.
